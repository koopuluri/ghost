# karthik's site preferences

These are persistent user decisions, not suggestions to re-decide during updates.
Read this file for any homepage, content or design change. AGENTS.md points here
and records the source-review and deployment workflow. Keep both current when
Karthik changes a preference; do not ask him to repeat decisions already recorded.

## page

One short Maipai paragraph followed by a chronological stream of title links to
original X posts, newest first. No visible dates or timestamps. Keep publication
dates in content/expressions.json for sorting and provenance.

No categories, topic headings, cards, descriptions below links, elaborate navigation,
chatbot, personalization, scheduled updates or runtime X/AI service.

## writing

All visitor-facing language is lowercase, extremely informal, playful and
unpolished. Keep it natural, not a joke on every line. No corporate/brand language,
em dashes, invented overarching thesis or chat invitation.

Maipai is karthik's ai, casually helping people keep track of what he's up to.
Describe actual recent activity provisionally; preserve uncertainty and contradiction.
Do not make exploratory thoughts sound like convictions or permanent traits.
Warmth and occasional light, specific exasperation are welcome.

The intro adds useful context; it must not merely restate titles or enumerate the
links below it. Avoid filler about how many posts are here. Specifically rejected:
“also, a video called ‘nothing special’. a small pile for now.” Don't bring this
pattern back when the archive grows. Keep the paragraph short (at most 90 words)
and editable separately in content/maipai.json.

## appearance

Simple, comfortable, text-first and mobile-friendly. Keep breathing room around
the page, with tighter spacing inside a paragraph. Current approved direction:
17px system sans-serif, line-height 1.4 (reduced from 1.7).

Links should look like familiar Google Docs links: blue (#1155cc), underlined,
including after visiting. A little space between consecutive links: 3px vertical
padding per row (6px between text line boxes), rather than the old 10px per row.
Keep keyboard focus obvious. No dates next to links, hero graphics or decoration.

## selection remains strict

Only expandable long text posts or original videos with verified text above them.
Exclude short text, ordinary images, textless videos, bare reposts, incidental
acknowledgments and ordinary replies. GIFs do not count as original videos.
Native X Articles are deferred pending a user decision; the existing rule has not
yet been broadened to include them. Prefer titles in Karthik's own words.
Record source evidence, exclusions and coverage gaps; never imply an incomplete
archive is complete. See AGENTS.md for thread handling and the complete update flow.
